# Cross-agency linking guidelines

Demo guidance for CrimeAI on Meko. It is not an official police document.

## Strong links
- The same payee UPI ID or beneficiary bank account in two FIRs is a strong link: the money went to the same place.
- The same caller phone number in two FIRs is a strong link, but rings rotate SIM cards every one to two weeks, so its absence proves nothing.

## Not links
- Public numbers (bank customer care, the 1930 helpline, courier customer care) appear in many FIRs because victims contact them. They must be excluded from linking.
- A shared method alone (two courier customs fee frauds, say) is a probable link only. An officer decides.

## Series
- A series is opened only when an officer confirms a proposed link. The agency with the earliest FIR normally leads.
- Once confirmed, the series is promoted on the grid, so every agency's intake agent recalls it and attaches new FIRs on intake.

## Privacy
- Agencies share identifiers only as fingerprints keyed with the grid key; the raw number never leaves the agency.
- Complainant details and narratives stay in the agency's private datapack.
