# Online financial fraud: first response SOP

Demo standard operating procedure for CrimeAI on Meko. It is not an official police document.

## Golden hour: stop the money
- Ask the complainant to report on the national cyber-crime helpline 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in immediately, if not already done.
- Send a debit-freeze request for the beneficiary UPI ID or bank account to the bank's nodal officer within the first hour; record the request time in the case.
- If the beneficiary identifier already belongs to a confirmed grid series, reuse that series' freeze request and inform the lead agency instead of filing a duplicate.

## Preserve evidence
- Collect the UPI transaction reference, payer and payee handles, chat screenshots and the call log with times.
- Note every customer-care or helpline number the complainant contacted; these are public numbers and are not suspect identifiers.

## Connect the case
- Recall the grid for every suspect identifier (phone, UPI ID, bank account) before closing intake.
- Agents only propose links. An officer confirms or rejects each one; confirmed series are promoted to every agency.
- The lead agency coordinates freeze requests and the chargesheet; other agencies attach their FIRs to the series.

## Courier or customs fee demands
- Customs never collects clearance fees over UPI from individuals. Treat any such demand as fraud.
- Ask whether the complainant was expecting a parcel, and record the courier brand the caller used.

## Bank KYC and OTP fraud
- Ask whether a screen-sharing app (AnyDesk, TeamViewer, QuickSupport) was installed; advise uninstalling it and changing banking passwords from a different device.
- Request the bank's transaction trail for IMPS and NEFT transfers made after the app was installed.
